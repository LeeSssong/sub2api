## Decisions made during integration

- Native configured Astra turn-state ownership wins on overlapping scope; new manual harvesting reports a configuration conflict instead of silently overriding it.
- Quality recovery requires unchanged owned revision/membership; unrelated edits may require manual confirmation, preserving manual disable safety.
- Request capture remains per-slot local storage. Directory flock fences writers even after database reconnect; cross-slot browsing/global quota is not implemented.
- Native release/update pipeline remains authoritative; source updater repository redirection is omitted to prevent loss of custom features.
- Known pre-existing unit-tag compilation and 9 account-creation frontend failures remain outside this fusion's directly affected behavior. Targeted new-feature and custom-feature regressions are reported separately.
